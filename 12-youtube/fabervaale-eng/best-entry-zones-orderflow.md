# How To Find The BEST Entry Zones

## Core idea
Best entry zones are reload areas where prior aggressive volume + big-trade clusters + value-area edges coincide (confluence). In a directional regime, after a breakout that *accepts* below a level, expect price to retrace and reload from that high-delta area in the trend direction — enter on absorption of the counter-side at that zone.

## Concrete techniques
- Stop placement is a tunable trade-off vs target: "1-2-3, 1-2-4, 1-2-5... depends how safe you want the stop" — i.e. wider stop above the zone = lower R but higher survival; explicit R:R/stop trade-off rather than a fixed rule.
- Trade *with* the winning side: read who got absorbed (failed buyers / failed sellers) and align entries to the side with results.
- Confluence beats single signals: value-area-high + big-trades + delta level stacking = "super strong area."
