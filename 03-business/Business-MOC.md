# Business-MOC

Hub for the business / revenue domain. Everything money-adjacent that isn't core Nexus trading or thesis work hangs off here. Smaller cluster than [[Nexus-MOC]] or [[Thesis-MOC]] by design — this is exploratory ground, not production.

## What lives here

- Productization ideas — could existing assets (Nexus, tooling, agents) become a product or service?
- Side-income streams — consulting, content, indie SaaS, etc.
- Pricing experiments — what to charge, how to position.
- Customer discovery — who would pay, what for, why.
- Tools + stack — concrete picks for delivery infrastructure.
- Decisions + bets — money/time commitments with explicit thesis.
